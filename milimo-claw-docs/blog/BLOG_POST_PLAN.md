## Proposed Structure

### Section 1: The Problem (Personal Hook)
- Open with a specific moment — finals week, a client deadline moved, someone's graduating, chaos ensues
- The real chaos of student freelancing: "The writer has to remind the designer the deadline moved"
- Why existing tools (Notion, Trello, Discord) require the very attention students don't have

### Section 2: Why AI Tools Don't Help
- ChatGPT is reactive — you prompt, it responds, it forgets
- Generic output from generic tools — doesn't know your client hates Oxford commas or your audience peaks Tuesday at 9pm
- No tool treats a friend group as a distributed business unit

### Section 3: The Insight
- Working with NemoClaw's architecture — realizing the sandbox isolation isn't just security
- "The OpenShell inter-sandbox gateway is the organizational chart"
- Each sandbox is a department. Each claw is a department head. The policy layer is the employment contract.
- **Key quote:** "You don't need to build a company. You deploy one."

### Section 4: What Milimo Claw Actually Is
- Six specialized claws: Content, Ops, Analytics, Finance, Build, Assistant
- Each claw runs on a different laptop in the squad
- They communicate through typed contracts — not chat
- The War Room: where humans stay in control

### Section 5: Self-Evolution (The Moat)
- This is what separates Milimo Claw from everything else
- Each claw runs a weekly evolution cycle: Observe → Identify → Propose → Build → Deploy
- Week 2 vs Week 36 claw is qualitatively different
- Cross-claw evolution: Analytics feeds Content, which builds tools Analytics couldn't build alone
- **Key insight:** A 9-month-old squad's claw has tools that don't exist at launch — built from their specific client mix, their specific audience

### Section 6: Privacy Architecture
- Financial data → Local NIM. Always. No exceptions.
- Source code → Local NIM. Never leaves device.
- This isn't policy. It's architecture. The privacy router enforces it transparently.
- Why this matters for students: real exposure if client data leaks

### Section 7: The Blueprint Economy
- When someone graduates, their evolved claw is exported to the next person
- Blueprints can be forked, merged, sold on a marketplace
- Institutional memory becomes tradeable asset
- The compounding moat: 9 months of evolution can't be purchased except by buying the blueprint

### Section 8: Why This Matters Now
- NVIDIA NemoClaw made this possible — multi-sandbox mesh + self-evolving agents + privacy router
- First product to exploit every layer of the stack for a consumer use case
- The gap between what students have (chaos) and what they need (autonomous coordination) is enormous
- "Milimo Claw is what happens when your squad's laptops form a company at 2am and keep running it forever."
